Support

Need help or found a problem? Open a support request. A free GitHub account is needed. Please don't include personal information; requests are public.

Adding a deck

RoadCards reads Anki .apkg files and CSV files with the question in the first column and the answer in the second. In Anki, turn on "Support older Anki versions" when exporting.

The voice sounds robotic

Open the Settings app, then Accessibility, find the section for reading and speech, open Voices, and download a voice marked Enhanced or Premium. RoadCards switches to it by itself.

Restoring a purchase

Open Settings in RoadCards and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account.

Cancelling

Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings on your iPhone.
