Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

RoadCards does not collect, store or share your personal data. The app has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Your decks and progress

Decks you import, your review history and generated audio stay on your iPhone. They are not sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes them.

Speech

Cards are read aloud using voices on your iPhone. Card text is not sent to any service by RoadCards.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Playback log

The app keeps a local playback log on your iPhone. It leaves your device only if you choose to share it.

Children

RoadCards is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions can be raised on the support page.
